### A mod for the 1.0 version of Call of Mini Dino Hunter, this mod aims to make the experience much more enjoyable and significantly more polished for everyone, below are the changes:

##


- **Rebalanced all of the stages**

- **Rebalanced all of the weapons**

- **Rebalanced all of the characters and their skills, with aro getting a full on rework**

- **Rebalanced the grind completely to be much shorter and more fair**

- **Rebalanced material sell prices and material drops**

- **Brought back the material drops system for dinos from the 0.5 version**

- **Added timed missions and also added melee only missions to the game**

- **Added around 12 new achievements with all of them including already existing ones now giving a fair amount of crystals too**

- **Reworked the elite dinos to be more deadly with more attacks and stronger attack variants**

- **Made the enraged bosses harder overall**

- **Added enraged bosses which are the new strongest dinos**

- **All dinos have a 1/500 change to drop a crystal material now that sells for 1 crystal with bosses always dropping 1-3 depending on the boss**

- **Added two new settings to the game**

- **Added more materials**

- **Added a new weapon to the game**

- **Added the ice map from the newer versions of the game**

- **Made the materials more visually consistent with their respective dinos and renamed a lot of materials too for consistency**

- **Completely overhauled the graphics**

- **Improved performance significantly**

- **Improved and added a decent amount of effects**

- **Improved the UI**

- **Improved the main menu**

- **Added a lot of new audio and added some of the unused sounds**

- **Made the audio a lot quieter overall for your own safety. It is no longer at a dangerous volume anymore**

- **Made some of the audio cleaner**

- **Made all of the music cleaner and added three new tracks**

- **Added the mike character from the later versions of the game**

- **Tons of bug fixes and small QOL changes/additions**

- *And more!*

## Here are all of the PC Controls:

- **Movement: WASD**
- **Attack: Left Click**
- **Toggle Cursor/Mouse Lock: Shift + F1**
- **Change Weapons: Q for previous weapon, E for next weapon**
- **Skill: CTRL/Middle Click**
- **Increase Sensitivity: O**
- **Decrease Sensitivity: I**
*These may be changed in a future update.*
